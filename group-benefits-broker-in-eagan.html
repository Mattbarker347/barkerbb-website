Serving Eagan Employers

A group benefits broker for Eagan companies.

Eagan has a lot of corporate square footage, and a lot of smaller companies working in its shadow. If your people could walk across a parking lot to an employer with a benefits department and a wellness budget, your package is being compared whether you like it or not.

Group benefits help for Eagan employers

We help Eagan employers build a package that reads well next to a corporate one, and that they can actually administer.

Benefits are a retention tool long before they are a recruiting one. The employee most likely to leave is not the new hire, it is the person five years in who just had a bad experience with a claim and concluded nobody was on their side. That experience is almost entirely about service, and service is the thing a smaller employer can genuinely win on.

We quote across every major Minnesota carrier so the plan itself is competitive, then we stay on it. When someone in your office has a denied claim or cannot get an ID card, they call us and a person picks up. Large employers frequently cannot say that.

Where a smaller employer actually wins

Response time

A benefits question answered the same day, by someone who knows your plan, beats a portal ticket every time.

Claim advocacy

When a claim gets stuck we get on the phone with the carrier. Your employee should not have to fight that alone.

Real enrollment meetings

We come in and explain the plan to your team in plain language, so people understand what they signed up for.

Plan design you chose

A large employer inherits a plan from corporate. You get to pick yours to fit the people you actually have.

Multi-state and remote employees

Eagan companies often have people outside Minnesota, and that changes the carrier conversation quickly. Network coverage outside the state varies a lot between carriers, and some plans that price well here leave a remote employee effectively uncovered. If you have staff in other states, tell us early. It narrows the carrier list, and it is much cheaper to know that before quoting than after.

We quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers, and we round the package out with dental, vision, life and disability, and supplemental coverage through Aflac. One conversation is usually enough to tell whether we can help.
