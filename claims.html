Common questions

The questions we get asked most, answered straight.

My claim was denied. Can you help?

Yes, and this is exactly when to call. We deal with the carrier on your behalf rather than handing you a phone number and wishing you luck.

What if my coverage isn't through Aflac?

It makes no difference. We place supplemental coverage through fifteen carriers as well as the major medical carriers, and we help with whatever you actually hold.

Do I need my policy number?

It helps and it is not required. Your name and employer is usually enough for us to find you and get started.
