Serving Eden Prairie Employers

A local group benefits broker for Eden Prairie businesses.

Eden Prairie is one of the metro's bigger business hubs, full of corporate offices, tech, and the steady stream of companies drawn to the southwest suburbs. Benefits shouldn't be the thing that slows any of them down, so that's the part we handle. We're a family-run Twin Cities agency that helps southwest metro employers build coverage aligned with the budget and the team.

Group benefits help built around Eden Prairie employers

Barker Brothers Benefits works with Eden Prairie small businesses to shop every major Minnesota carrier, model the real options side by side, and land on a plan that fits where the company is now and where it's going.

Health coverage costs keep climbing, and sorting it out alone is genuinely hard. That's where we come in. We're independent, so we're not tied to a single insurance company, and we quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers so you can compare real numbers instead of guessing.

Eden Prairie teams tend to be well paid and benefits-literate, which changes the conversation. These employees read the plan documents, ask about the out-of-pocket maximum, and know what an HSA is. That is an opportunity, because the strategy that fits them is often cheaper for you too.

High-deductible plans and health savings accounts

Lower premium, funded account

A high-deductible plan costs less in premium. Redirecting part of that saving into employee HSAs often buys more goodwill than the richer plan did.

The tax treatment is genuinely good

HSA money goes in pre-tax, grows, and comes out tax free for medical costs. It is the employee's account and it follows them.

Who it does not fit

A team with chronic conditions or predictable high claims can be worse off. We look at your census before recommending it.

Employer contribution strategy

Funding the account monthly, annually, or matching all send different messages. The choice matters more than the amount.

Who we work with

Eden Prairie and southwest metro employers from a couple of employees up to around two hundred, from growing tech and office companies to the service businesses around Eden Prairie Center. Whether it's your first plan or a renewal that came back higher than it should have, we can help you sort it out.

Growing fast without breaking the plan

Tech and corporate employers in the southwest metro often add people in bursts, and a benefits plan set up for the size you were can quietly stop fitting the size you are. New hire waiting periods, eligibility definitions, and the point at which the ACA employer mandate applies all deserve a look before a growth year rather than after it.

However we get there, the process is the same five steps for every group we take on. You can read it on our locations page.

Beyond medical, we round out the package with dental, vision, life and disability, and supplemental coverage through Aflac, so your team is covered for what a health plan alone tends to miss.
