Common questions

The questions we get asked most, answered straight.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing to get a quote, and nothing extra on your premium. Carriers build broker compensation into the rates either way, so the real question is whether you have somebody working for you rather than whether you are paying for one.

How we work

Are we too small for a group plan?

Probably not. Group coverage in Minnesota reaches down to very small employers, and participation matters more than headcount. A business with a couple of employees can have a real plan.

How quickly can coverage start?

Usually a few weeks from the first conversation. Carriers work to effective dates on the first of a month, and enrollment paperwork takes real time, so starting earlier gives you more options.

Do you only do health insurance?

No. Medical, dental, vision, life, disability and supplemental coverage, plus the enrollment technology and the employee meetings that make a package actually get used.
