Compare Your Options

The trade-offs, side by side.

Most benefits decisions come down to a fork in the road. These pages lay out both sides honestly, including the case against the option we usually recommend.

Every one of these comparisons is a real conversation we have had with an employer, usually more than once. Should we go level funded or stay fully insured? Is ICHRA actually simpler, or does it just move the work? Does anyone use a whole life product in a group setting, and if so, who?

We wrote these to be genuinely two-sided. A comparison page that concludes every time that you should do the thing the broker gets paid more for is not a comparison, it is an ad. Where one option is usually better we say so and explain why. Where it depends, we tell you what it depends on, so you can look at your own numbers and decide.

None of these replace running your actual census through the market. They just mean you walk into that conversation knowing what the words mean.
