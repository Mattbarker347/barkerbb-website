Common Questions

The questions employers actually ask us.

These are the real questions, in the words people use when they call. No jargon, no hedging, and no pretending the answer is simple when it is not.

Benefits is a field that runs on acronyms and fine print, which is exactly why most employers feel behind when they start asking questions. Nobody should have to know what an SPD is before they can find out whether they need one. So these pages answer the question first, in plain language, and then explain the reasoning underneath it.

A lot of them are compliance questions, because that is where the real risk sits. Whether you have to offer coverage at all, whether your plan meets ACA affordability, what a Section 125 plan does for pre-tax deductions, when you can add or drop an employee. Getting these wrong is expensive in a way that a slightly suboptimal plan design never is.

If your question is not here, it is a good question and we would like to hear it. That is what the phone is for.
