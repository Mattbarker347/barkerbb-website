Twin Cities and Greater Minnesota

Where we work across Minnesota.

We are a family-run agency based in Ramsey, and we work with employers all over the Twin Cities metro. Pick your city below for the local page, or just call us. The service is the same wherever you are.

Being local matters more in benefits than people expect. Carrier networks are regional, provider access differs by county, and a plan that works beautifully in Woodbury can leave a Maple Grove team driving across the metro for care. We quote every major Minnesota carrier, so we can weigh those trade-offs against where your people actually live.

It also means we show up. If your enrollment meeting is in Eden Prairie on a Tuesday morning, we are in the room. If an employee in St. Paul has a claim stuck, they call us and a person picks up. That is the whole pitch, and it is easier to deliver when you are down the road rather than in another state.

How it works, wherever you are

Five steps, the same for every group we take on.

  1. We learn your businessSize, budget, what is working, and what your people actually use.
  2. We shop the marketWe take your census to every major carrier and pull back real quotes with a few plan designs.
  3. We bring you a short listTwo or three options we would actually recommend, in plain English, with the trade-offs spelled out.
  4. We handle the rolloutCarrier paperwork, the enrollment system, and the meeting with your team.
  5. We stay onClaims questions, coverage issues, and next year's renewal, which we start months ahead.

Common questions

The questions we get asked most, answered straight.

Do you cover my city if it isn't listed?

Almost certainly. These are the cities we publish a page for, not the limit of where we work. We serve employers across Minnesota and Wisconsin.

Do you actually come out in person?

Yes, for enrollment meetings and for renewals. Being in the room is the part that most brokers have quietly stopped doing.

Are you licensed in Wisconsin too?

Yes, and we work there actively rather than occasionally. Some carrier rules differ by state, so we flag which ones apply to you.
