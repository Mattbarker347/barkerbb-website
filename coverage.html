Coverage and Carriers

Every line we place, and who we place it with.

A benefits package is rarely one product. These guides cover each line of coverage on its own terms, plus the Minnesota carriers we quote for it.

Most employers start with medical because it is the biggest line item and the loudest one at renewal. But the coverage employees actually use week to week is often dental, vision, or a supplemental accident plan that pays cash when someone breaks an ankle. Building a package means deciding which lines you fund, which you offer voluntary, and where the budget does the most good.

We are independent, which means we are not captive to one insurance company. We quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, Delta Dental, Beam, MetLife, and Aflac, plus alternative structures like ICHRA and the Health Access Solutions cooperative. Each carrier has plans it is genuinely good at and plans it is not, and the only way to know is to put real quotes side by side.

The coverage guides explain what each line does. The carrier pages cover what that specific company is strong at in Minnesota.
