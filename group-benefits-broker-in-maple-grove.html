Serving Maple Grove Employers

A local group benefits broker for Maple Grove businesses.

Maple Grove is one of the fastest-growing corners of the metro, full of retail around Arbor Lakes, healthcare, and the trades and family-run shops that come with a community still on the rise. Benefits shouldn't be the thing that slows you down, so that's the part we take off your plate. We're a family-run Twin Cities agency that helps northwest metro employers build coverage aligned with the budget and the team.

Group benefits help built around Maple Grove employers

Barker Brothers Benefits works with Maple Grove small businesses to shop every major Minnesota carrier, model the real options side by side, and land on a plan that fits where the company is now and where it's heading.

Health coverage costs keep climbing, and figuring it out alone takes time a growing business can't spare. That's where we come in. We're independent, so we're not tied to a single insurance company, and we quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers so you can compare real numbers instead of guessing.

Full medical coverage is not always where a growing business should start. For a lot of retail, trades, and family-run shops, the honest first step is a package employees will genuinely use, at a number the business can carry every month without flinching.

Starting with dental and vision

Employees use it

Dental and vision get used every year by most people, which makes them the most visible benefit per dollar you will ever offer.

Predictable cost

Rates are stable and low compared to medical, so budgeting is straightforward and renewal surprises are rare.

A real recruiting line

On a job posting, dental and vision is meaningfully better than no benefits, and candidates notice.

Build from there

Starting here does not close any doors. Adding medical later is a normal next step, not a restart.

Who we work with

Maple Grove and northwest metro employers from a couple of employees up to around two hundred, from the retail and service businesses around Arbor Lakes to the trades and growing shops that are adding their first real benefits. Whether you're starting from scratch or your renewal just jumped, we can help you make sense of it.

Growing communities hire fast

A business in a growing part of the metro tends to add people in a hurry, and benefits paperwork is the thing that slips when you are busy. Waiting periods that fit your actual hiring pattern, and someone else handling the enrollment forms for every new hire, keeps that from turning into a backlog of people who should have been covered a month ago.

However we get there, the process is the same five steps for every group we take on. You can read it on our locations page.

Beyond medical, we round out the package with dental, vision, life and disability, and supplemental coverage through Aflac, so your team is covered for what a health plan alone tends to miss.
