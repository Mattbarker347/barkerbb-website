Serving Apple Valley Employers

A health insurance broker for Apple Valley businesses.

Apple Valley's business base runs small. Professional practices, service companies, local shops, and offices where the owner is also the HR department, the payroll department, and the person who fields the question about why a claim was denied. Benefits are one more hat, and usually the least favorite one.

Group benefits help for Apple Valley employers

We take the benefits hat off small Apple Valley employers, which for most owners is the entire point of using a broker.

Small groups have a real advantage that gets overlooked. Decisions are fast, there is no committee, and the owner knows every employee's situation personally. That means a plan can be chosen to actually fit the people on the census rather than an abstract average, which is a luxury a five hundred person company does not have.

The disadvantage is time. Nobody starting a business planned to spend a week comparing deductible structures. So we do that part, bring back two or three options we would genuinely recommend, and explain the trade-offs in a conversation rather than a spreadsheet.

If you have never offered benefits before

Plenty of Apple Valley employers reach us at the point where an employee has asked about health insurance for the first time, or a good candidate turned down an offer over it. Starting from nothing is a normal place to start. Under fifty full-time equivalents you are generally not required to offer coverage at all, so the question is whether it makes business sense for you, and that is a conversation worth having before anyone quotes you anything.

We quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers, and we round the package out with dental, vision, life and disability, and supplemental coverage. One conversation is usually enough to tell whether we can help.
