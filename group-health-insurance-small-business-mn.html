Common questions

The things employers ask us most about this, answered straight.

Do I have to offer health insurance to my employees?

If you have fewer than 50 full-time equivalent employees, no federal rule requires it, and Minnesota doesn't add one of its own. At 50 or more, the ACA employer mandate applies. Most of the smaller employers we work with offer coverage to compete for good people, not because they're forced to.

How many of my employees have to enroll for a plan to issue?

Carriers set a minimum share of eligible employees who have to take the plan, and the number varies by carrier. People who waive because they're covered somewhere else, like a spouse's plan, usually don't count against you. It's worth knowing the threshold before you shop, because it shapes what's actually available to you.

What does group health insurance cost for a small business in Minnesota?

There isn't a single per-employee price. Minnesota small group medical is age banded, so your number depends on the ages of your team, your rating area, and the plan design you pick. That's why we ask for a census before quoting rather than giving you a figure we'd have to walk back. There's no charge for the quote.

Can I change carriers in the middle of the plan year?

Usually you'd change at renewal, and that's the cleanest path. A mid-year move is possible and sometimes right, but it resets deductibles your team has already worked through, so it's worth talking out before you commit to it.
