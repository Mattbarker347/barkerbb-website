Group benefits help for Coon Rapids employers

We are your neighbors in Anoka County, and we build benefits for the kind of companies that actually operate here.

Physical work changes what a benefits package should do. When your people are on their feet, around equipment, or on job sites, the coverage that gets used most is not always the medical plan. Accident coverage, short-term disability, and hospital indemnity pay cash at exactly the moment an hourly employee's income stops, and they cost a fraction of medical premium.

That does not mean skipping medical. It means not spending the entire budget on medical richness while leaving the gap that actually shows up unfunded. We look at what your workforce is likely to claim and build outward from there.

Coverage that fits physical work

Accident coverage

Pays cash on an injury off the job, where workers comp does not apply. Heavily used in trades and manufacturing.

Short-term disability

Replaces part of a paycheck when someone cannot work. For an hourly employee this is the difference between a setback and a crisis.

Hospital indemnity

A cash benefit on admission, which softens a high deductible without paying for a low-deductible plan.

Straightforward medical

A solid plan people understand, rather than a complicated one that looks good on paper and confuses everyone.

Shift work and enrollment

Getting a full crew into an enrollment meeting is genuinely hard when they do not all work the same hours. We run multiple sessions, meet people on their shift, and sit one-on-one with anyone who wants to go through it privately. Enrollment participation is not just an administrative number, it drives your rates, so it is worth the extra trips. We make them.

We quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers, and we round the package out with dental, vision, life and disability, and supplemental coverage through Aflac. One conversation is usually enough to tell whether we can help.
