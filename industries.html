Industry Guides

Benefits built for your industry.

A restaurant with heavy turnover and a manufacturer with twenty-year employees need very different benefits packages. These guides cover what actually matters in each industry.

Industry shapes almost everything about a benefits plan. Turnover decides whether your waiting period should be thirty days or ninety. Workforce age drives which carriers come back competitive. Hourly versus salaried changes what counts as full-time under the ACA, and whether a supplemental accident plan is a nice-to-have or the most-used benefit you offer.

We have built plans across manufacturing floors, construction crews, restaurant groups, dental and medical practices, senior living operators, property management companies, insurance agencies, professional services firms, and tech companies. The guides below cover the specifics: the participation problems that show up, the coverage that gets used, and the plan designs that hold up at renewal.

If your industry is not listed, it does not mean we cannot help. It just means we have not written the page yet. Call and ask.
