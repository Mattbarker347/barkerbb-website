Serving Blaine Employers

A group benefits broker for Blaine businesses.

Blaine is another Anoka County neighbor, a short drive from our office in Ramsey. It has grown quickly, with newer commercial and industrial space filling in alongside long-established local businesses, and those two groups tend to arrive at benefits from opposite directions.

Group benefits help for Blaine employers

Whether you are setting up benefits for the first time or fixing a plan that has drifted for years, we start in the same place: what your people actually need.

A newer company usually has no plan and no idea what one should cost. An established one usually has a plan nobody has examined in years, renewed on autopilot, with a broker who has not visited since the day it was sold. Both are fixable, and both start with an honest look at the current situation rather than an immediate pitch.

Sometimes that look concludes you should stay exactly where you are. We will say so. A broker who tells you to switch every single year is not reading your situation, they are reading their own.

Two ways employers get here

Starting from nothing

We explain what is required, what is optional, and what it realistically costs, before anyone talks about carriers.

Inheriting a stale plan

A plan renewed on autopilot for years is usually leaving money or coverage on the table. Often both.

An unresponsive broker

The most common reason employers call us. Service is the whole product in this business.

A bad renewal

A double-digit increase is a reason to shop, not a reason to panic. There are usually more options than presented.

What a second opinion actually involves

It is not a sales meeting. We look at your current plan, your census, and your renewal, and we tell you whether what you have is competitive. If it is, that is the answer and it costs you nothing. If it is not, we show you specifically where, and you decide what to do about it. Most employers have never had anyone check, which is why so many stale plans stay stale.

We quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers, and we round the package out with dental, vision, life and disability, and supplemental coverage through Aflac. One conversation is usually enough to tell whether we can help.
