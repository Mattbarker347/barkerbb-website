Serving St. Louis Park Employers

A group benefits broker for St. Louis Park employers.

St. Louis Park sits right against Minneapolis, which means employers here compete for staff with downtown firms while running on a smaller back office. You get the recruiting pressure of the city without the HR department to match it. That gap is the part we fill.

Group benefits help for St. Louis Park employers

We help St. Louis Park employers put together benefits that hold up against a downtown offer, without needing a benefits person on staff to run them.

An inner-ring suburb has a particular hiring problem. Your candidate is also interviewing somewhere with a full HR team and a benefits portal, and the coverage line on the offer letter gets compared directly. Being smaller does not have to mean being outmatched here, because plan design and carrier choice do most of the work, and those are available to a fifteen-person company on the same terms as a large one.

We are independent, so we quote across every major Minnesota carrier and put the options next to each other in plain numbers. Where a smaller employer usually loses is not the plan, it is the time it takes to compare plans properly. That is our job, not yours.

Competing for staff without an HR department

The offer-letter comparison

We help you understand how your package actually reads against a larger employer's, and which one or two changes close most of the gap.

Contribution strategy

How much you fund, and for whom, moves participation and perception more than the plan itself. We model it before you commit.

Ancillary as a differentiator

Dental, vision, and a supplemental line often do more for a candidate's read on an offer than another point of medical richness.

Someone to call

Your team gets our number, not a queue. For a company without an HR desk, that is the benefit administration.

Renewal season without the scramble

Most St. Louis Park employers we talk to first reached out because a renewal came back higher than expected and there were three weeks to decide. We start renewals months ahead so the decision is made on real alternatives instead of under a deadline. If you are in the scramble right now, that is still a fine time to call, it just means we move faster.

We quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers, and we round the package out with dental, vision, life and disability, and supplemental coverage through Aflac. One conversation is usually enough to tell whether we can help.
