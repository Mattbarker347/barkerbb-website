Common questions

The questions we get asked most, answered straight.

What happens after I send this?

A real person reads it and replies within one business day. No call center, no automated sequence.

What do you need from me to quote?

A census and a rough budget. That means dates of birth and zip codes for who would be enrolling, not Social Security numbers, and we never ask for medical details to run a quote.

Yes. We work actively in both Minnesota and Wisconsin, which matters if your team crosses the border.
