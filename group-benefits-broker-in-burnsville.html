Serving Burnsville Employers

A group benefits broker for Burnsville businesses.

Burnsville runs on a mix that does not fit one benefits template. Retail and service businesses with hourly staff and real turnover, sitting alongside light industrial and trades companies where people stay for twenty years. Those two need almost opposite plans, and plenty of Burnsville employers have both under one roof.

Group benefits help for Burnsville employers

We build benefits for Burnsville employers whose workforce is not one group, and where a single plan design leaves somebody out.

A mixed workforce is the most common reason a benefits package quietly underperforms. Offer one rich plan and your hourly staff decline it because their share is unaffordable, so participation drops and your rates suffer. Offer one cheap plan and your long-tenured people feel it as a downgrade. Both failures look like the same thing on the renewal: enrollment lower than it should be.

The fix is usually structure rather than spend. A second plan option at a different price point, a contribution formula that flexes, or a voluntary line that gives lower-paid staff something real without the medical premium. We model these against your actual census, not a rule of thumb.

When one plan does not fit everyone

Two-tier plan design

A richer option and a genuinely affordable one, so nobody is choosing between coverage and rent.

Participation math

Carriers require a minimum enrollment. We check your design against it before you offer it, not after it fails.

Hourly and salaried together

Waiting periods, eligibility, and full-time definitions that work for both groups without creating a compliance problem.

Voluntary lines that carry weight

Accident and hospital coverage cost employees little and pay cash when something happens. For hourly staff they often matter more than a deductible.

Turnover is a benefits problem too

If you are re-enrolling a meaningful share of your staff every year, administration stops being a once-a-year task. We handle the enrollment meetings, the carrier paperwork on new hires and terminations, and the employee questions that would otherwise land on whoever answers the phone at your office. That is not an add-on service, it is the part of the job that never ends.

We quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers, and we round the package out with dental, vision, life and disability, and supplemental coverage through Aflac. One conversation is usually enough to tell whether we can help.
