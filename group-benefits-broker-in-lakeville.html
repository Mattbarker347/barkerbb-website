Serving Lakeville Employers

A group benefits broker for Lakeville businesses.

Lakeville has grown fast, and a lot of its employers grew with it. Companies that were six people a few years ago are twenty now, and benefits decisions that made sense at six start creating problems at twenty. Crossing certain headcounts changes what you are allowed to do and what you are required to do.

Group benefits help for Lakeville employers

We help growing Lakeville employers get benefits right at their current size, and see the next threshold coming.

Headcount thresholds are the part of benefits that catches growing companies off guard. Some carriers price differently above and below certain group sizes. Some plan structures stop being available. And at fifty full-time equivalents the Affordable Care Act's employer mandate applies, which turns benefits from a choice into a requirement with real penalties attached.

None of that is hard to handle if you see it coming. It is expensive if you find out during an audit. When we look at a growing group we look at where you are now and where you will be in eighteen months, and we say plainly which decisions you should make once rather than twice.

What changes as you grow

Approaching 50 employees

The ACA employer mandate applies at 50 full-time equivalents. Part-time hours count toward that math, which surprises people.

Carrier tiers by size

Group size affects which carriers will quote you and how they rate you. Growth can genuinely improve your options.

Level funding becomes available

At a certain size, a level funded plan can return money in a good claims year. It is not for everyone, and we will tell you if it is not for you.

Formalizing what was informal

A handshake benefits arrangement that worked at eight people becomes a compliance exposure at twenty five.

Industrial and trades employers

A good share of Lakeville's employment sits in manufacturing, distribution, and the trades, and those workforces have specific needs. Physical work means accident and disability coverage get used, not just offered. Shift schedules make enrollment meetings harder to run. And a workforce that skews toward long tenure often values a strong medical plan over the extras. We build for that rather than assuming an office template.

We quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers, and we round the package out with dental, vision, life and disability, and supplemental coverage through Aflac. One conversation is usually enough to tell whether we can help.
