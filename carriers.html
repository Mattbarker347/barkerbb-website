Carriers

We are independent, so you get the whole market.

We are not captive to one insurance company, which means we quote your group across the carriers below rather than the one shelf we happen to sell. Which carriers make sense depends on your team, your budget, and which doctors and dentists your people already use.

Medical

Traditional group medical, level funded plans, and the cooperative alternative.

Dental

Group and voluntary dental, from the large Minnesota networks to the newer carriers.

Vision

Group and voluntary vision, including the vision-only carriers most employers ask for by name.

Supplemental

Accident, critical illness, hospital indemnity, and short and long term disability.

Life

Employer paid basic life and voluntary term life.

Minnesota Paid Leave

Private plan options for Minnesota Paid Family and Medical Leave.

Not sure which of these fits your team?

That is the whole job. Send us a census and we will shop it and come back with two or three real options.
