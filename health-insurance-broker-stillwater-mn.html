Serving Stillwater Employers

A health insurance broker for Stillwater businesses.

Stillwater's business base leans toward independent operators, hospitality, and professional offices, with a seasonal rhythm that a lot of the metro does not have. Staffing up for the busy season and back down after it makes benefits eligibility a moving target, and the rules do not bend just because your headcount does.

Group benefits help for Stillwater employers

We help Stillwater employers handle benefits when the size of the team changes with the calendar.

Seasonal variation creates a specific compliance problem. Whether an employee counts as full-time, and whether your business counts as a large employer under the ACA, both depend on hours measured over time rather than headcount on a given day. Businesses that swing seasonally can cross those lines without noticing.

There are measurement methods designed exactly for this, and used properly they let you offer coverage consistently without accidentally owing it to your entire seasonal roster. Used improperly, or not at all, they are where penalties come from. It is worth twenty minutes to get set up correctly.

Benefits when headcount moves

Measuring full-time properly

Hours are averaged over a defined period. Getting the method right is what keeps seasonal staffing from becoming a mandate problem.

Eligibility and waiting periods

A waiting period set correctly handles most seasonal churn without any special administration.

Year-round core staff

Your permanent team should not get a watered-down plan because of seasonal complexity around them.

Being on the river

Employees living or working across the state line raise real network questions. Worth checking before you choose a carrier.

Independent businesses and owner coverage

In a town of owner-operated businesses, how the owner is covered is not a footnote. Eligibility and tax treatment vary by entity type, and some supplemental products treat owners differently than employees. If you are an S corp, a partnership, or a sole proprietor with staff, say so early. It changes the answer, and it is one of the most common places we find a previous setup done wrong.

We quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers, and we round the package out with dental, vision, life and disability, and supplemental coverage. One conversation is usually enough to tell whether we can help.
