Level funding, and whether it fits you

How it differs

You fund expected claims plus stop-loss protection. In a good year the unused claims money can come back to you rather than staying with the carrier.

The catch worth knowing

In a bad year your costs are capped by stop-loss, but they can run higher than a fully insured premium would have. It is a real trade.

Who it suits

Stable headcount, a reasonably healthy group, and an employer with the cash flow to absorb a variable month.

Who it does not

Very small groups, volatile headcount, or a business that needs one identical number every month. We will say so plainly.

Who we work with

Minnetonka and southwest metro employers from a couple of employees up to around two hundred, from established professional firms to smaller shops setting up benefits for the first time. Whether you're new to offering coverage or your renewal keeps climbing, it's worth a second set of eyes.

Getting claims data you can actually use

One underrated advantage of these arrangements is visibility. Fully insured groups get almost no information about what is driving their costs. With more reporting you can see whether spending is concentrated in a few claims, in pharmacy, or in avoidable emergency room use, and that turns your renewal into a conversation about specifics instead of an argument about a percentage.

Common questions from Minnetonka employers

What comes up most when we sit down with a business here.

What is level funding?

A middle ground between fully insured and self funded. You pay a set amount each month, and the plan's actual claims decide whether money comes back at the end of the year.

Fully insured against level funded

Is level funding right for us?

It depends on the size and stability of your group and your tolerance for variability. It rewards a healthier than average group and it punishes a volatile one, so it is not automatically the cheaper choice.

Would we get claims data?

Usually more than a fully insured plan gives you, which is one of the real advantages. Data you can act on is what turns the next renewal into a decision rather than a surprise.
