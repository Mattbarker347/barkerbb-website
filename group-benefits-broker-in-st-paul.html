Health coverage costs keep climbing, and working through it alone is tough. That's where we come in. We're independent, so we're not tied to a single insurance company, and we quote across Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, HealthPartners, Medica, UnitedHealthcare, and the regional carriers so you can compare real numbers instead of guessing.

East metro businesses often have people who have been there a long time, which is a good problem with a real cost attached. Group medical is age-banded, so a workforce that has aged together sees rates climb even in a year when nobody made a claim.

An experienced workforce and what it does to your rates

Age-banded rating

Most Minnesota small group medical is priced by employee age. A stable, long-tenured team gets more expensive every year by design.

It is not your claims

Employers often assume a rate increase means somebody had a bad year. Frequently it is just the census aging into the next band.

Where the levers actually are

Plan design, contribution strategy, and carrier choice. Shopping the market matters more for an older group, not less.

Retirement-age employees

Once staff approach Medicare eligibility the coordination rules matter, and getting them wrong is expensive for the employee.

Who we work with

St. Paul and East metro employers from a couple of employees up to around two hundred, whether you're putting a plan together for the first time or your renewal just landed with an increase you weren't expecting. Both are good reasons to bring in a second set of eyes.

Long-tenured teams expect more from a plan

Somebody who has been with you fifteen years notices when coverage gets worse, and they tell everyone. That makes changing carriers a communication job as much as a purchasing one. When we move a group with long tenure, we sit down with the team and show them exactly what changed and what did not, because a good plan people do not understand still lands as a downgrade.

However we get there, the process is the same five steps for every group we take on. You can read it on our locations page.
