Benefits Glossary

Open enrollment

The window each plan year when employees can join, drop or change coverage without needing a qualifying event.

Open enrollment is the annual window when anyone eligible can make an election. Outside it, changes generally require a qualifying life event. For a group plan the window is tied to the plan year, not to the calendar.

How long it should take

For a small group, a couple of weeks of active enrollment is usually enough, with the decisions made before that. The work that determines whether it goes smoothly happens earlier: rates confirmed, materials ready, and a meeting scheduled where people can ask questions.

Treat it as the one moment you have

It's the only point in the year when you have everyone's attention on benefits. A group that runs a real meeting gets higher participation, which can improve guaranteed issue amounts on voluntary products and helps satisfy carrier participation requirements. Emailing a packet and hoping does none of that.

Quick Facts

  • FrequencyOnce per plan year
  • Tied toThe plan year, not the calendar
  • Outside itA qualifying event is needed
  • DrivesParticipation, and what it unlocks

Related terms

S

Special enrollment

A limited window outside open enrollment when an employee may change coverage because of a qualifying life event.

Q

Qualifying life event

A change in circumstances, like marriage or losing other coverage, that lets an employee change their benefits outside open enrollment.

M

Minimum participation

The share of eligible employees a carrier requires to enroll before it will issue or renew a group plan.
