Benefits Glossary

Explanation of benefits (EOB)

The statement a carrier sends after processing a claim, showing what was billed, what the plan paid, and what the member owes.

An EOB is not a bill. It's the carrier's accounting of a claim: what the provider charged, what the negotiated rate was, what the plan paid, and what's left for the member.

The line that matters

The gap between the billed amount and the allowed amount is the network discount, and it's often the largest number on the page. It's the clearest evidence an employee will ever see of what staying in network is worth.

Why employees call about it

EOBs arrive before the provider's bill and they say things like "this is not a bill" in small print. People assume they owe the amount at the top. Walking a team through one real EOB at an enrollment meeting prevents a year of phone calls.

Quick Facts

  • Is it a bill?No
  • Sent byThe carrier
  • ShowsBilled, allowed, paid, member owes
  • ArrivesUsually before the provider bill
